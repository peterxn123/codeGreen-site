What is codeGreen?

codeGreen is a beginner-friendly hackathon for secondary schoolers where coders of all kinds will spend 12 hours together to build a project based on our theme - tech that will help us in our nation's target of reaching net zero. Our event works to encourage participants (could be you!) to improve their coding skills and collaborate with people with all kinds of talents.

FAQ

Who can participate?

Anyone 18 or under passionate about technology, regardless of experience level!

What should I bring?

Your laptop, charger, and enthusiasm! We'll provide food, drinks, and workspace.

Is there a participation fee?

The event is completely free for all participants.
